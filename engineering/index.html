12-week sprints
Give us three months and we'll bring your software engineering group up to industry best practices. We focus on the industry proven techniques, tools and procedures your team needs most. Our OODA-loop program is a structured training program that deeply embeds powerful R&D support engineering culture into your group.
The OODA-loop program consists of a combination of in-person and remote sessions delivered weekly, over 12 weeks. Participants learn the principles of the most successful R&D software build and support.
Through role plays, worksheets, templates, quizzes and homework.
Count on intense participation (2-3 hours per week for each consultant) and extraordinary, long-lasting impact on your experimental processes.

Group Coaching
Fuel your engineering group's success by creating new superstar engineers. We work with small teams of your high-potential coders and biologists to unleash their experimental automation potential.
Typically we work with 3-4 person peer groups or project teams. We maintain an intense focus on team efficiency, and bringing known effective techniques from software driven industries into the lab space.
Group coaching uses your current, in-lab experiments as our training ground. The focus is tactical and pragmatic, not innovative. The dollars saved in raw materials and person-hours are real too.

Faster Experimentation Session
Identify what's really slowing your team down. Our intensive data-based Experiment Risk Lab details exactly where you need to focus to increase the yield and reduce the failed experiments.
During our 1.5 days together, we dive deep into your engineering and biologist teams workflow, creating depth of understanding and partnership, and total clarity on the scientists goals.
Then, our comprehensive, 140-question model benchmarks your team against 19 drivers of feature delivery, reliability, and data integrity.
You’ll leave our session with deep, exciting insights about your engineering team and a prioritized, detailed road map to growth.
