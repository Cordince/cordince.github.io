Data Pipelines for Biologists
Embark on a journey with a select group of biologists and biomedical engineers specializing in software solutions for biotechnology. Engage in a dynamic, collaborative, and virtual workshop designed to revolutionize your data management strategies.
No matter your expertise in biotech, you'll be infused with innovative ideas, motivated to excel, and equipped with a comprehensive plan to navigate the next 12 months of your experimental data pipeline.
During the Data Pipeline Acceleration Program, you and your peers will delve into every facet of your data management practice. From refining your data collection methods to integrating cutting-edge tools and techniques, you'll be on the express path to enhancing your research outcomes.
It's energetic. It's empowering. It's educational. It's the most engaging and productive group experience for software engineers and biologists in the biotech field.
Plus, it's the most thorough and data-driven approach to feature delivery you've ever seen. You'll learn techniques born from building photonics products from the ground up, and from working with tissue engineers developing processes that grow human tissue.

Version Control and Traceability
Consistently increase the quality of your data acquisition process by adding version control and traceability techniques and procedures. We understand the complexities of managing experimental data and can show you how to build in:
  • A robust, efficient pipeline for handling your experimental data
  • Relief from the uncertainty of managing complex datasets
  • A strategic plan for data management that's proven effective for biologists
  • The resource and time preserving benefits of a streamlined data management system
  • Increased confidence in your data integrity and reproducibility
  • Comprehensive knowledge of data management best practices...and the tools to implement them
This includes live Q&A sessions with Nathan plus access to an online vault filled with pipeline-building lessons.

Automation Without Complication
You have almost everything you need to automate your experiements without adding variability and confusion. – you’re just missing a couple of critical ingredients. Ingredients you will learn in this program.
In this online program you’ll learn how to:
  • Create tight loops around the data collection and evaluation steps.
  • Ensure you're recording the right data at the right rates.
  • Build automated regression tests so you know that as the experimental protocols change, your data collection won't break.
  • Provide traceability at all stages in the data pipeline to ensure scientists have full confidence in the recording process.
  • Add events and alerts, saving your experiments from failures and your scientists from tedious lab monitoring.
